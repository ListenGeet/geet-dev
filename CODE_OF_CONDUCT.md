# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Geet's community to be welcoming and respectful to everyone, regardless of background or identity. Participation in project spaces should be free from harassment and personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient and respectful in discussions and reviews.
- Critique ideas and code constructively; do not attack people.
- Accept feedback and make room for different experiences and viewpoints.
- Respect the privacy of others. Do not publish personal information or private communications without consent.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, deliberate disruption, and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers using GitHub's contact or reporting tools. Reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
