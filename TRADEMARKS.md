The code in this repository is licensed under AGPL-3.0-only (see `LICENSE`). That licence does not cover the Geet brand.

## What is not licensed

- The name "Geet" and "listengeet.com".
- The wordmark and the logo (the yellow disc with five bars, and the bars-only mark).
- Everything in `web/public/brand/`, `web/public/icons/`, `web/public/favicon.*`, `web/public/og/` and `web/public/email/`.
- The five illustrations in `web/public/illustrations/` and the sign-in sound in `web/public/audio/`.

These files stay in the repository because the site needs them to run. You may read and build them as part of running this code for your own review or development. You may not use them to present a service as Geet.

## If you fork or run your own copy

- Use a different name and a different logo.
- Replace the files listed above before you publish a running service.
- Do not suggest that your service is made, endorsed or hosted by Geet.

## What you can do without asking

- Say that your project is based on the Geet source code, in plain text, and link to this repository.
- Refer to Geet by name when you write about it, review it or report a bug.
